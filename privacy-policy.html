Section A — Privacy Policy

KIDDIE CLOUD ("we", "us") operates the KIDDIE CLOUD mobile application, a nursery discovery platform for parents in Kuwait. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

To confirm

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and phone number when you create a parent or nursery account.
  • Bookings and visit requests: the date, time, and nursery you select.
  • Reviews and ratings you choose to submit.
  • A device token used to send you notifications about your bookings and account.
To confirm

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to operate the app, connect you with nurseries, process visit bookings, respond to support requests, and improve the service.

3. Third-Party Services

KIDDIE CLOUD uses Firebase (a Google service) to help operate parts of the app.

To confirm

4. Sharing of Information

Nursery owners can see booking and review information submitted to their own nursery profile. We do not sell personal information to third parties.
